Partner Program Terms

Last updated: November 11, 2026
Draft. Company details will be added before sales start.

The partner program lets you share a personal link and earn a reward for pre-orders made through it.

1. Who can join

Anyone aged 18 or over with a valid email address.

2. Rewards

  • You get a reward for each paid order placed through your link: a percentage of the order in money and months of subscription, as shown on the partner page.
  • Rewards count only after the order is paid in full and not cancelled or returned within the return period.
  • Money rewards are paid by Wise or another method we agree on, after the order ships. You are responsible for any taxes on your rewards.

3. Rules

  • No spam, misleading claims about the product, fake orders or orders to yourself.
  • No paid ads that bid on the Inner Ear name.
  • We may cancel rewards gained by breaking these rules and close the account.

4. Changes

We may change or end the program with 14 days’ notice by email. Rewards already earned stay valid.
