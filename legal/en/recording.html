Recording & Consent

Last updated: November 11, 2026
Draft. Company details will be added before sales start.

Inner Ear records the conversations around you. Laws on recording differ between countries and US states, and you are responsible for following the ones that apply to you.

1. One-party and all-party consent

  • In most US states and in many countries, you may record a conversation you take part in (one-party consent).
  • Some US states, including California, Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Montana, New Hampshire, Pennsylvania and Washington, require everyone in a private conversation to agree to recording.
  • In the EU, UK and other places, recording other people can also be limited by privacy law.
  • This list is a short guide, not legal advice. Check your local rules.

2. How Inner Ear helps

  • The device is visible and shows when it is recording. There is no hidden or remote recording mode.
  • Only Me mode builds insights only from your own voice. Other people’s speech is not analysed for patterns or stats.
  • You can delete any recording at any time.

3. Good practice

  • Tell people when Inner Ear is on, especially at work, at home with family and in private conversations.
  • Turn it off when someone asks you to, and in places where recording is not allowed.
  • Never use Inner Ear to record anyone secretly, to follow or control someone, or for any illegal purpose.
